# Speaker notes: Sample Coffee Q3 2030 business review

## How to use these notes
- Lead with the conclusion on every page, then the evidence.
- Keep the whole deck inside 8 minutes.

## Time budget
| Section | Pages | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | 1–4 | 4 min |
| Main line | 5–6 | 4 min |

### Page 1｜Cover
**Duration**: 20 s
**Talking points**:
Hello everyone. Today I will walk through the third quarter. All figures are synthetic demo data.
**Transition**: Let us start with the agenda.

### Page 2｜Agenda
**Duration**: 30 s
**Talking points**:
Two parts today: the conclusion and revenue, then cost and next steps.
**Transition**: The conclusion first.

### Page 3｜Conclusion: Q3 revenue up 12% year on year
**Duration**: 1.5 min
**Talking points**:
Revenue grew 12% year on year, mostly from the 3 new stores.
Same-store growth was 4%, slightly slower than last quarter.
**Transition**: In the revenue mix, delivery keeps taking share.
**Likely questions**:
- Q: How much came from the new stores? A: About two thirds of the increase.
- Q: Why did same-store growth slow down? A: Mainly weaker foot traffic in the catchment area.

### Page 4｜Revenue mix: delivery up to 38%
**Duration**: 2 min
**Talking points**:
Delivery went from 34% to 38%.
Note that delivery gross margin fell 2 points after the platform raised its commission.
**Transition**: Now the cost side.

### Page 5｜Cost: green coffee purchase price up 9%
**Duration**: 2 min
**Talking points**:
Green coffee is up 9%, so ingredient cost went from 28% to 30% of revenue.
**Transition**: Given these changes, we plan three things for Q4.
**Likely questions**:
- Q: Can we switch supplier? A: In talks, but quality needs validation.

### Page 6｜Next steps: three things for Q4
**Duration**: 1.5 min
**Talking points**:
First, lock in next year's green coffee contract; second, raise the delivery set price by 1 yuan; third, pause new stores and lift productivity in the existing ones.
**Transition**: That is everything. Comments welcome.
